# Astronaut Health Copilot

**Astronaut Health Copilot** is a health monitoring and medical support software designed for astronauts on space missions. Whether crews are aboard a space station, inside a transit spacecraft, or operating on the lunar surface, this platform helps track vitals, assess symptoms, and manage medical emergencies in real time.

## Key Features

### Flight & Vitals Overview
* **Mission Telemetry:** Tracks journey progress, mission distance, days to arrival, and Earth signal delay.
* **Environmental & Biometric Tracking:** Monitors radiation exposure, sleep time, Heart Rate Variability (HRV), and reaction times.
* **Data Visualization:** Displays historical health and mission metrics through an interactive chart.

### Daily Checkup & Reaction Test
* **Guided Checkups:** Prompts astronauts to complete daily health checkups and provides step-by-step instructions on how to use onboard medical devices.
* **Cognitive Assessment:** Includes a built-in reaction test to monitor neurological alertness and fatigue levels over time.

### Symptom Triage
* **Interactive Pain Mapping:** Allows astronauts to select the exact location on the body where they feel pain or discomfort.
* **Targeted Diagnostics:** Guides the user through taking the specific physiological measurements required for that symptom.

### Emergency Alert System
* **One-Touch Emergency Button:** Instantly triggers an audio siren and flashes red visual alarms along the edges of the screen to alert nearby crew members during a critical medical event.

### Medical Library
* **Space Medicine Reference:** Familiarizes astronauts with common spaceflight health risks and key symptoms to watch for.
* **Actionable Protocols:** Outlines common contributing factors, immediate steps to take, and clear instructions on how fellow crew members can assist.

### Crew Records
* **Centralized Health Logs:** Securely stores and organizes individual health profiles, checkup histories, and diagnostic data for all crew members.


## Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** [React, Tailwindcss, HTML, CSS, Typescript]

## Disclaimer
*This software is a prototype/educational project intended for mission simulation and health tracking concepts. It is not certified for actual flight operations or clinical medical diagnosis.*
